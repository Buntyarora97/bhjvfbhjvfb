The Beauty Ritual

🧼
Step 1 — Cleanse

Wash your face thoroughly with a gentle cleanser and pat completely dry to prepare skin for oil absorption.

✨
Step 2 — Apply

Take 2–3 drops of Kumkumadi Beauty Oil onto your fingertips and distribute evenly on the face and neck.

Step 3 — Massage

Gently massage with your fingertips in slow upward circular motions for 1–2 minutes for deeper penetration.

🌙
Step 4 — Leave Overnight

For best results, leave the oil overnight. Skin absorbs the herbal goodness during its natural repair cycle.

💡 Pro Tip (Night Routine): Apply consistently every night before bed. Results are most visible with daily 6–8 week use. Avoid direct sunlight immediately after application.
